# Lumen Metrix — Brand Identity Guidelines

## Brand Essence

**Lumen** (Latin: *light*) represents clarity, insight, and illumination — turning raw church data into actionable understanding. **Metrix** signals precision, measurement, and analytical rigor. Together, the name promises *light through measurement*.

**Brand Positioning:** A modern church analytics platform that feels warm and approachable for a solo pastor, yet professional and scalable enough for a 10,000-member multi-site organization.

**Brand Personality:** Warm, clear, confident, precise, quietly premium.

---

## Logo System

### Logo Mark (Icon)
The Lumen Metrix icon is an abstract geometric form that merges a **rising bar chart** with a **light ray / prism refraction**. Three ascending bars fan outward from a common origin point, evoking both upward growth metrics and light refracting through a prism. The angles suggest forward momentum and illumination.

### Wordmark
"LUMEN" is set in **DM Sans Bold** with generous letter-spacing (tracking +0.08em), giving it a confident, modern presence. "METRIX" follows in **DM Sans Medium** at the same size, creating a subtle weight contrast that distinguishes the two words without breaking visual unity.

### Logo Configurations
1. **Full lockup** — Icon + Wordmark (horizontal, primary use)
2. **Stacked lockup** — Icon above wordmark (square contexts)
3. **Icon only** — Favicon, app icon, small spaces
4. **Wordmark only** — Text-heavy contexts

### Clear Space
Minimum clear space around the logo equals the height of the "L" in LUMEN on all sides.

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity over decoration** — Every element earns its place. No gratuitous gradients, no ornamental flourishes.
2. **Warm precision** — Data is presented with mathematical accuracy but wrapped in warm tones and generous spacing.
3. **Progressive disclosure** — Show the headline first, detail on demand. Respect the executive's time.
4. **Quiet confidence** — The brand doesn't shout. Premium quality speaks through restraint, not excess.

## UI Patterns

### Cards
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with `1px` border `#E8E5DE`
- Border radius: `8px`
- Shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)`
- Padding: `20px`

### KPI Cards
- Left border accent: `3px` in campus or metric color
- Value: DM Mono 500, 28-32px
- Label: Inter 500, 10px, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.08em

### Buttons (Primary)
- Background: `#E8913A`
- Text: `#FFFFFF`
- Border radius: `6px`
- Hover: `#C47A2E`

### Navigation
- Sidebar for SaaS context (persistent left nav)
- Tab bar for dashboard sections
- Active state: Lumen Amber underline or fill

## Voice & Tone

- **Clear, not clever** — "Your attendance grew 14% this year" not "Attendance is on fire!"
- **Confident, not boastful** — State facts, let the data speak
- **Warm, not casual** — Professional but never cold or corporate
- **Concise** — Every word earns its place, just like every pixel
