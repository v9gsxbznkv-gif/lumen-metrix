# National Church Giving Per Capita Benchmarks (Per Person Per Week)

## Key Data Points (2024-2025 sources)

### Christian Standard (July 2025) — Church Report Survey
Based on combined in-person + online attendance:
- **Overall national average: $29.63/person/week**
- Megachurches (2,000+): $22.39/person/week
- Emerging Mega (1,000-1,999): $39.29/person/week
- Large (500-999): $43.77/person/week
- Medium (250-499): $43.22/person/week
- Small (100-249): $42.20/person/week
- Very Small (<100): $42.19/person/week

Based on in-person attendance only:
- **Overall: $39.08/person/week**
- Large: $53.34
- Medium: $49.67
- Small: $48.35
- Very Small: $46.18

### Vanco (2025) — 25,000 churches
- Average giving per person in 2022: $2,848/year ≈ $54.77/week
- Smaller churches (~50 attendees): $2,589/year ≈ $49.79/week
- Larger churches (5,000+): $2,340/year ≈ $45/week

### Nonprofits Source
- Average giving by adults in US Protestant churches: ~$17/week

### Subsplash (2025)
- Average church attendee gives less than $20/week

### Church Health Wiki (older data)
- Average weekly per capita giving (WPCG) in American church: $26
